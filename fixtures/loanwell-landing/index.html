How it works

One night,
start to soil.

Toss in food scraps at night. In the morning, thermal drying and natural microbes turn them into nutrient-rich soil in as little as 24 hours.

24hcompost cycle
01ADD

Load it after dinner.

Toss in your food scraps — fruit peels, veggie ends, coffee grounds and more. No sorting, no mess, no hassle.

02CYCLE

Press start, go to bed.

Our quiet system dries and breaks down scraps with natural microbes. It runs while you sleep, so there’s no smell and no work for you.

In as little as 24 hours, food scraps become nutrient-rich soil. Use it for houseplants, a herb garden, or your community garden.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What can I put in Loanwell One?+

Fruit and vegetable scraps, coffee grounds, eggshells and many everyday food scraps. Refer to the final product guide for a complete accepted-material list.

Does it smell?+

The proposed design uses multi-stage carbon filtration to help control odors during the compost cycle.

How loud is it?+

Loanwell One is designed for quiet countertop operation, with a listed target sound level of 28 dB.

How long does a cycle take?+

A cycle is designed to take approximately 12–24 hours, depending on the materials and amount added.

Where do I use the soil?+

Use the finished material for houseplants, herbs and outdoor gardens as directed by the product instructions.

How often do I need to empty it?+

It depends on how much you add. Empty the container after a finished cycle and before it reaches its recommended capacity.

What if I live in a small space?+

Loanwell One is designed with a small footprint for apartment kitchens, offices and other compact living spaces.

Is there a warranty?+

The reference offer mentions a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. Full warranty terms should be confirmed before any real purchase.
